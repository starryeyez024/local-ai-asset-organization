Quick reference

Pick the step you need. Ask your assistant to read the linked skill and follow it.

  1. Create local AI configuration — agent-setup.
  2. Build or update personal context — context interview.
  3. Audit your assets — ai-asset-auditor.
  4. Add skill symlinks — skill-linker.
  5. View your skills — open the Explorer, or ask “Open my Skills Explorer.”
  6. Refresh your catalog — ask “Refresh my skill catalog.” Setup and refresh instructions.
  7. Customize a shared skill — skill-override-safeguard.
  8. Check tool support — compatibility chart.
